# Auto-Sales-Business-Intelligence

### Project Overview
End-to-End Auto Sales Business Intelligence project using Python, SQL and Power BI to analyze sales perfomance, products, customers, markets, pricing and business trends.

### Dataset
- **source** Kaggle Auto sales Dataset
- **Rows** 2747 records
- **Period** 28, August 2026 to 29, September 2026
- **Columns** 20 original columns

## Key Findings

1. Sales changed significantly across the analyzed years increasing in sales from 2018 to 2019 and dropping sales in 2020
2. Classic cars generated the largest share of recorded sales
3. Sales were geographically concentrated with USA having the most average sales. The top three countries together contributed approximately 58.2% of the sales.
4. The largest customer, Euro Shopping Channel, generated approximately $912k in sales
5. Medium sales deals represented the largest share of revenue.
6. November had the highest sales across all the years.
7. Most associated sales were associated with shipped orders, approximately 92.4%

## Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following areas could be considered for further business investigation:
### Product Perfomance
Investigate the factors behind the strong contribution of Classic Cars and determine whether similar patterns exist across individual products and markets
### Geographic Markets
Examine the concetration of sales across countries and cities to understand market dependancy and opportunities for broader geographic coverage.
### Customer Concetration
Monitor the contribution of high-value customers and investigate customer retention and purchasing patterns.
### Seasonlity
Investigate the reccuring November sales peaks and deterimine whether seasonal campaigns, purchasing cycles or other factors may explain the pattern
